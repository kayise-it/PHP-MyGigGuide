## What you get

1. **Branded 91.9 FM apps** — Android (already in progress) + **iPhone** (required).
2. **Live stream** + **WhatsApp** as the main listener tools (other features can stay in the app).
3. **Web subdomain** — e.g. `919fm.mygigguide.co.za` — 91.9-branded **viewing** of the gig guide / site.
4. **Hosting** on My Gig Guide’s VPS (may need a capacity upgrade; setup fee can help with that).

**Web access (important):**  
91.9 FM staff get **view / browse** access on the subdomain.  
**Not** included under this outline: website roles such as presenter login, admin exec, advertising manager, or other station CMS roles. If that is needed later, we discuss it separately.

**My Gig Guide platform:**  
Dave may keep a **mini 91.9 FM** presence inside the **vanilla My Gig Guide** app (On Air / station strip — as already built), so MGG listeners can find and open 91.9. That stays part of this partnership.

## Who does what

### 91.9 FM (station)

- Pay the **agreed one-off setup fee**.
- Send **stream URL** details and keep them up to date.
- Send **WhatsApp** number / link and keep it up to date.
- Send **logos**, colours, and store listing text / screenshots you want used.
- **Approve** store listings before we go public.
- Nominate **one contact** for day-to-day questions.
- Confirm we may use 91.9 as a **reference** and in **agreed** My Gig Guide promotion (logo / name use as you approve).
- Handle **listener** WhatsApp and on-air relationships (we don’t moderate your chats).

### My Gig Guide (Dave)

- Finish / ship the **91.9 branded apps** (Android + iPhone).
- Wire **stream** + **WhatsApp** from what you supply.
- Set up the **919 subdomain** for **view-only** website use.
- Host the tech (and upgrade the VPS if we need to for reliability).
- Help with **Google Play / Apple** packaging and submission as agreed.
- Fix **broken stream / WhatsApp** issues on a **best-effort** basis (no monthly retainer).
- Keep the **mini 91.9** entry in vanilla My Gig Guide.
- Only use your brand in marketing as **agreed**.
