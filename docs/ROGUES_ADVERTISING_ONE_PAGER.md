# Rogues on Radio — advertising & platform services (one-pager)

**Between:** Rogues on Radio (station operator)  
**And:** Kee Consulting Pty Ltd (platform provider — My Gig Guide technology)

**Purpose:** Phase 1 pilot — sponsored placement on the **Rogues app** and **rogues.mygigguide.co.za** only.  
**Status:** Draft for discussion — figures negotiable.

---

## What Kee Consulting provides

- White-label **Rogues** mobile app (Android; iOS when ready)
- **rogues.mygigguide.co.za** website (events, map, listings)
- **Advertising delivery** — two launch placements (home + events list), clearly labelled **Sponsored**
- **Station portal** (roadmap) — approve ads, listener tools, polls, reporting
- Hosting on shared platform infrastructure (VPS scaled as more silos join)
- Technical support and product maintenance

---

## What Rogues provides

- **Sales** to local advertisers (“advertise on Rogues on Radio / our app”)
- **Merchant of record** — Rogues invoices the advertiser and collects payment (EFT / invoice)
- **Creative approval** — Rogues approves every banner before it goes live (Phase 1)
- Monthly **ad sales report** to Kee Consulting (campaign name, gross amount, dates)

---

## Fees (proposed)

| Item | Amount | Notes |
|------|--------|--------|
| **Platform maintenance fee** | **R 5 000 / month** (negotiable) | App + site + ad system + hosting footprint; covers scaling headroom as more brands join |
| **Revenue share** | **20% of gross ad revenue** | Applied to paid third-party campaigns Rogues sells; invoiced monthly by Kee Consulting |
| **House / station promos** | Excluded from rev share | Rogues promoting own shows/station — no platform fee on those unless agreed otherwise |

**Example:** Rogues sells one “Rogues Spotlight” package for R 3 000. Kee Consulting receives R 600 rev share (+ monthly maintenance separately).

---

## Launch ad product (Phase 1 pilot)

**Package name:** Rogues Spotlight (30 days) — *retail price set by Rogues*

| Placement | Where |
|-----------|--------|
| **Home spotlight** | Banner on Rogues home (web + app) |
| **Events sponsored row** | One labelled slot in the events list |

- All creatives labelled **Sponsored**
- Rogues approves creative before activation
- Kee Consulting activates campaign in platform after Rogues confirms payment + approval

---

## Workflow (Phase 1)

1. Rogues agrees sale with advertiser → Rogues invoices advertiser  
2. Advertiser pays Rogues → sends banner + link to Rogues  
3. Rogues approves creative  
4. Rogues notifies Kee Consulting → campaign set live for 30 days  
5. End of month: Rogues reports gross ad sales → Kee Consulting invoices maintenance + rev share  

---

## Term & pilot

- **Initial term:** 3 months pilot, then review  
- **Exclusivity:** Platform tech via Kee Consulting / My Gig Guide for this white-label instance  
- **Data:** Listener/ad stats owned by platform; Rogues receives reports for their silo
