# Claim your page — guide for venue owners & artists

One account works on **[mygigguide.co.za](https://www.mygigguide.co.za)** and the **My Gig Guide app**.

---

## Venue owners — claim your venue page

### What “claiming” means

Your venue may already be listed (name, address, gigs). **Claiming** links that listing to **your** account so it shows as official and you can manage it.

### Before you start

1. Find your venue on [mygigguide.co.za](https://www.mygigguide.co.za) (search or map).
2. Note the **contact email** on the listing (shown on the claim banner if we have one).
3. You’ll need a **free My Gig Guide account** using that same email (or you’ll need the manual route below).

### Option A — Your email matches the listing (fastest)

**On the website**

1. Open your venue’s page.
2. If it says **Unclaimed**, use the amber **“Claim this page”** banner.
3. **Create account** or **Log in** with the **same email** as the venue listing.
4. **Verify your email** (check inbox for the verification link).
5. After verification, the page is linked to you automatically.

**In the app**

1. Sign in with the **same email** as the venue listing.
2. If we find a match, a **“Claim your page”** banner appears — tap **Claim now**.
3. Approved pages appear under **Me → Pages**.

### Option B — Your email does *not* match the listing

Use this if the listing has an old/wrong email, or you use a different address.

**In the app only (for now):**

1. Sign in.
2. Open your venue’s page in the app.
3. Tap **Request to manage this page**.
4. Add a short note (optional) — e.g. “I’m the manager; contact email is outdated.”
5. Our team reviews it. You’ll see **pending review** until it’s approved.

We’ll email you when it’s approved or if we need more info.

### After you’re approved

- Your venue shows as **Official** on the site and app.
- **Me → Pages** lists venues you manage.
- On the **website**, sign in and use your **dashboard / edit venue** to update photos, description, etc.
- Anyone (including you) can **post gigs** at your venue via **Add event** in the app.

## Artists — claim your artist page

### What “claiming” means

Your band or stage name may already have a profile. **Claiming** links it to **your** account so fans see an official page tied to you.

### Before you start

1. Find your **artist page** on the site or app (Explore / search).
2. Check the **contact email** on the listing.
3. Create or use an account with that email — **one artist page per account** for the automatic route.

### Option A — Your email matches the listing (fastest)

**On the website**

1. Open your artist page.
2. Use the amber **“Claim this page”** banner if the page is **Unclaimed**.
3. **Register** or **Log in** with the **same email** as the artist listing.
4. **Verify your email**.
5. The page links to you after verification.

**In the app**

1. Sign in with the **same email** as the artist listing.
2. Tap **Claim now** on the banner if it appears.
3. Check **Me → Pages** for your artist profile.

### Option B — Your email does *not* match

**In the app:**

1. Sign in.
2. Open your **artist page**.
3. Tap **Request to manage this page**.
4. Explain briefly why you should manage it (optional message).
5. Wait for admin approval — status shows as **pending review**.

### After you’re approved

- Your page shows as **Official**.
- It appears under **Me → Pages**.
- Edit your profile on the **website** (bio, photos, links).
- **Post gigs** from the app (**Add event** / Explore) and link yourself as the artist.

**Note:** Adding yourself to a gig from the **map** isn’t supported — use **Explore / Add event** for that.

## Quick comparison

| | **Venue** | **Artist** |
|---|-----------|------------|
| **Automatic claim** | Account email = listing **contact email** | Same |
| **Where to claim (easy)** | Website banner or app **Claim now** | Same |
| **Wrong email?** | App → **Request to manage this page** | Same |
| **How long?** | Usually instant after email verify; manual = few days | Same |
| **See your pages** | **Me → Pages** (app) or dashboard (web) | Same |

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
|---------|------------|
| No claim banner | Page may already be official, or no contact email on the listing — use **Request to manage** or contact support. |
| “No listings match your email” | Sign-in email ≠ listing contact email — use manual request or ask us to update the listing email. |
| Claim stuck on **pending** | Verify email on the website; or wait for manual review. |
| Already have another artist page | Only **one** artist page per account on the automatic path — contact us if you manage multiple acts. |
